Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026. This policy covers the ArtRaccoon website and the Pinterest integration "RaccoonArtis Repost" (the "App").

1. Who uses the App

The App is a private tool operated by ArtRaccoon for its own Pinterest account (RaccoonArtis). It is not offered to the public; no other person can sign in to it or connect their account.

2. Data we access

  • The Pinterest OAuth access and refresh tokens of our own account, granted through Pinterest's official OAuth flow.
  • Scopes: boards:read (list our own boards to choose where to pin), pins:read (check our own existing Pins to avoid duplicates), pins:write (create Pins with our own artwork), user_accounts:read (confirm which account is connected).
  • Our own images and captions from our Telegram channel t.me/ArtRaccoon.

3. Data we do not collect

We do not access, collect or store any data about other Pinterest users: no profiles, followers, messages, comments or analytics about people. This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

4. Storage and security

Tokens and a list of Pin IDs created by the App are stored in private, access-restricted storage available only to the owner. They are never logged publicly and are used solely to publish our own Pins.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, advertisers or data brokers.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the App is in use. Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest Settings → Security and logins → Apps. On revocation or on request we delete stored tokens and Pin records within 30 days.

7. Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
